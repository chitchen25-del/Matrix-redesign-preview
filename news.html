Scaling for Global Demand: Commissioning Our Second Rubber Extrusion Line

Driven by extraordinary global growth and an unyielding commitment to our conversion partners, Matrix Engineering is thrilled to announce a major operational expansion at our Ballasalla manufacturing facility on the Isle of Man. We have officially commissioned our second state-of-the-art micro-cellular rubber extrusion line.

"This strategic investment immediately doubles our production capacity for Exceed Rubber, ensuring seamless, uninterrupted factory-direct supply chains for our global network of die-makers and carton converters."

Uncompromised Quality at Scale

As packaging converters face tighter deadlines and higher press speeds, the reliability of ejection material is more critical than ever. Our signature Exceed Rubber is engineered to maintain structural integrity across more than 1.6 million impressions without degradation. This secondary extrusion line not only secures our current output capability but also lays the technical foundation for an upcoming suite of specialized profiles launching later this year.

The British Manufacturing Advantage

By keeping 100% of our extrusion tooling, polymer compounding, and quality assurance in-house on the Isle of Man, Matrix Engineering maintains absolute control over microscopic tolerances. Our partners can continue to rely on rapid lead times, zero supply chain friction, and world-class British manufacturing standards.
