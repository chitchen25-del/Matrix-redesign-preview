Mastering the Makeready: 5 Proven Strategies to Eliminate Press Downtime

In high-speed folding carton conversion, profit margins are won and lost during makeready cycles. Every minute a press sits idle adjusting crease channels or troubleshooting cracking board is revenue lost. Achieving micro-accurate setup on the first impression is the hallmark of a world-class plant.

Key Operational Takeaways

1. Exact Caliper Calculation

Always match channel depth precisely to board caliper using reliable engineering formulas rather than guesswork.

2. Eliminate Platen Creep

Utilize high-shear, clean-peel adhesive systems that prevent matrix shifting under extreme tonnage.

3. Standardize Locator Bridges

Employ thermally bonded self-locating bridges to center matrix perfectly over rule lines instantly.

4. Leverage Digital Tools

Use online sizing calculators to instantly verify dimensions before cutting material strips.
