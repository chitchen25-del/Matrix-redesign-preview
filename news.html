Matrix Engineering Powers Global Growth with Commissioning of Second Advanced Rubber Extrusion Line

BALLASALLA, ISLE OF MAN — August 24, 2026

Driven by record international demand and continuous global expansion across the folding carton and die-cutting sectors, Matrix Engineering is thrilled to announce the successful implementation and commissioning of our second state-of-the-art rubber profile extrusion line at our Isle of Man manufacturing facility.

Scaling Capacity to Meet Surging Global Demand

Over the past two years, packaging converters and tooling houses across the globe have increasingly turned to Matrix Engineering’s signature Exceed™ Rubber line. Engineered to maintain its dynamic profile and elasticity over 1.6 million impressions without degradation or angel hair, the Exceed™ range has rapidly set a new standard for pressroom durability.

To support this accelerating worldwide adoption, our substantial investment in a second dedicated extrusion line significantly expands our daily production capacity. This milestone guarantees:

  • Shorter Lead Times: Expedited order turnarounds for high-volume container shipments worldwide.
  • Factory-Direct Reliability: Uninterrupted supply chain security directly from our British Isles manufacturing hub.
  • Microscopic Tolerances: Advanced digital inline monitoring to ensure every millimetre of rubber meets rigorous shore hardness and profile consistency standards.

Pioneering the Next Generation of Profiles

This expansion is about far more than just scaling volume—it fuels our relentless commitment to product innovation. Equipped with advanced multi-zone temperature controls and high-precision die-tooling capabilities, this new line empowers our in-house engineering and R&D teams to work hard on accelerating development for a brand-new suite of cutting-edge profiles.

We will be bringing custom geometries, bespoke converter solutions, and new sustainable formulations to market in the near future.

"This second extrusion line is a direct testament to the trust our global partners place in Matrix Engineering products every day. By reinvesting in our Isle of Man production infrastructure, we are ensuring our converting partners have the dependable, high-precision tools they need."

With our production capacity doubled, a rich pipeline of breakthrough profile products nearing launch, and our international client base expanding rapidly, the future at Matrix Engineering has never looked brighter.
