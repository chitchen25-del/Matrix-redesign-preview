EXCEED™ RUBBER Micro-Cellular Ejection

Exceed rubber comes from a combined experience of over 40 years of design and production of profile extrusion. Maintaining its exact profile for over 1.6 million impressions, it provides a highly cost-effective solution.

  • Zero Degradation: Higher running life with absolutely no reduction in performance after 1.6 million+ impressions.
  • Eliminates Angel Hair: Flawless sheet breakaway from the cutting rule.
  • Resilient Formulation: Excellent UV resistance and high resistance to heat generated from press friction.
  • Faster Setup: Highly dimensionally stable for greatly reduced make-ready time.
