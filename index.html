Operating Procedure

6-Step Matrix Installation

Follow our standardized process to ensure optimal channel transfer and rule durability.

STEP 01

Clean Plate Surface

Thoroughly degrease and dry the cutting plate or counter matrix jacket using standard solvent degreaser.

STEP 02

Cut Matrix to Size

Cut the matrix strip cleanly to length using a dedicated 45° or 90° matrix hand/bench cutter.

STEP 03

Locate onto Rule

Fit the locator channel bridge firmly over the creasing rule in the die.

STEP 04

Peel Adhesive Backing

Peel the protective finger-lift backing tape to expose the high-tack adhesive layer.

STEP 05

Press Impression

Cycle the press through one impression under standard pressure to transfer the matrix onto the counter plate.

STEP 06

Remove Locators

Remove the plastic locator bridges from the channel. You are now ready for production.
