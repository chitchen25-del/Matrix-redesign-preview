Who we are

Built on precision, run by people who make the stuff

Matrix Engineering is an independent manufacturer supplying creasing matrix and ejection rubber to folding carton converters and die-makers worldwide.

Over 60 years of combined experience

Our production lines were designed and built in house on the Isle of Man, pairing specialist extrusion equipment with materials we specify ourselves. We're proud of the British manufacturing behind it, and of supplying converters across the globe direct from the factory.

Synthetic matrix for folding carton

Phoenix+

Our standard matrix, and the one most presses run

Phoenix+ is where our polymer and adhesive work ends up. It is built for long, high-speed runs on folding carton, and it is the range we hold most sizes of on the shelf.

Available on 7mm mini, 7mm off-centre, 10mm standard and 12mm bases, in centre and double crease.

What it gives you

Key features

Clean-peel tape

Holds under pressure, lifts without residue, no cleaning the platen between jobs.

Self-locating bridges

Small enough for tight areas and thermally bonded, so nothing is left behind in the channel.

Virgin polymer

Not regrind. It is what gives the channel walls their run life.

Smooth shoulder

Clean sheet transfer without reworking the shoulder by hand.

Colour coded by height

Printed on the base and the locator, so the right one is obvious on the bench.

Polyester base, steel-compatible

Ultra-SR

A steel replacement that cannot rust

Ultra-SR is a high-impact polyester base at the same thickness as steel matrix, so it drops straight into a steel specification. On abrasive recycled board it holds crease depth without rusting or leaving perforation marks.

An allowance of 0.20 should be considered, as with any steel-based product.

What it gives you

Key features

Formulated adhesive

Specified for maximum impressions rather than for easy manufacture.

Steel compatible

Same thickness as steel matrix, so existing specifications carry across.

No rust, no perforation

The two things that end a steel matrix's life on recycled board.

Fingerlift tape as standard

On every size, for faster makeready.

On the bench

Fitting matrix in six steps

  1. Clean the plate

    Degrease and dry the cutting plate or counter jacket. Anything left on it will show up in the crease.

  2. Cut to length

    Use a proper matrix cutter at 45° or 90°. A clean end sits down flat.

  3. Locate on the rule

    Push the locator bridge down over the creasing rule so the channel sits square.

  4. Peel the backing

    Lift the fingerlift tape to expose the adhesive, keeping the strip in place.

  5. Take an impression

    One cycle at normal pressure transfers the matrix onto the plate.

  6. Remove the bridges

    Lift the locators out of the channel and you're ready to run.
