Standard Operating Procedure

6-Step Matrix Installation

Follow our standard process to achieve perfect channel alignment and maximum bond strength.

STEP 01

Clean Plate Surface

Thoroughly clean and degrease the cutting plate or counter matrix jacket using standard solvent degreaser.

STEP 02

Cut to Size

Cut the creasing matrix strip to length using a dedicated 45° or 90° matrix hand/bench cutter.

STEP 03

Locate onto Rule

Fit the matrix channel locator strip firmly onto the creasing rule in the cutting die.

STEP 04

Peel Adhesive Film

Peel the protective backing tape from the matrix base to expose the high-tack adhesive layer.

STEP 05

Press Impression

Cycle the press through one impression under standard pressure to transfer the matrix onto the plate.

STEP 06

Remove Locators

Remove the plastic locator bridges from the matrix channel. You are now ready for production.
