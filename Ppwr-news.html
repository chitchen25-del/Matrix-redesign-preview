Matrix Engineering Achieves Full PPWR 2026 Compliance

August 2026

Leading the Charge in Sustainable Supply Chains

August 2026 marks a monumental shift in the global packaging landscape. With the European Union’s Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) now officially in application, the standard for environmental responsibility has been permanently raised.

At Matrix Engineering, we believe that world-class creasing performance should never come at the expense of our environment. We are incredibly proud to announce that our entire packaging operation is now fully PPWR compliant. By auditing our systems well ahead of the rolling deadlines, we are guaranteeing that our global network of carton converters and die-makers receives the same uncompromising quality, housed in packaging that is radically leaner, greener, and entirely future-proof.

Engineered to Minimize

The core philosophy of the PPWR is that the best way to handle waste is to never create it in the first place. We have fully embraced the new strict minimization rules for both weight and volume.

Every Matrix Engineering box leaving our facility has been meticulously optimized. We have eliminated all avoidable empty space, ensuring that we use only the absolute minimum amount of material necessary to protect your products during global transit.

Total Transparency & Traceability

Compliance isn't just about what you see; it's about the data backing it up. Securing this milestone required a top-to-bottom optimization of our entire supply chain. We have collaborated extensively with our dedicated cardboard box suppliers to audit raw materials and compile comprehensive PPWR Technical Files.

This rigorous downstream verification means every piece of packaging that arrives at your factory floor is backed by verified conformity assessments. You can trust that our packaging footprint is traceable, transparent, and built to the highest European standards.

100% Safe, 100% Recyclable

As part of our compliance, we have ensured our packaging lifecycle is entirely free from restricted substances of concern. We stand fully aligned with the newly enforced bans on PFAS chemicals, ensuring our packaging is as safe as it is efficient.

Furthermore, by strictly controlling the materials entering our facility, we guarantee that our dispatch packaging is highly recyclable, keeping materials in the economic loop and out of landfills.

Looking Forward

Achieving PPWR compliance is a massive operational milestone, but our commitment to innovation doesn't pause here. We are already mapping out our strategies for the EU's strict 2030 design-for-recycling targets. Matrix Engineering remains steadfast in our mission: delivering the precision tooling you rely on, in the sustainable packaging the future demands.
